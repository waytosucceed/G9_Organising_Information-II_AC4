Sample Writing

Pets are some of the most selfless creatures on the planet. They give and give and don’t really expect anything back. Although, to be fair, it’s not like they can talk, but even then, language is not a barrier when it comes to communication. While we do love our pets and care for them like our family, we are indeed more dependent on them than they are on us. If someone says pets are a bundle of joy and a warm hug, I won’t hold it against them, because it is true.

Writing Activity:

Pick a topic from the following list and write a passage about it:

  • Mothers are self-sacrificing.
  • Pets are absolutely the best.
  • Maths is a weird subject.
  • Playing a [sport] is fun.
  • Preparing for a test.

Word Limit: 50-100
